# Dataset Sources, Provenance, and Licensing

TrustGuard AI enforces strict data governance, research compliance, and provenance tracking across all multimodal verification models.

---

## 1. Primary Image Benchmark: TrustGuard Multi-Source V4 Dataset

- **Directory**: `training/datasets/deepfake_v4_multisource/`
- **Total Images**: 10,000 verified samples
- **Splits**:
  - **Train**: 7,000 images (3,500 REAL, 3,500 FAKE)
  - **Validation**: 1,500 images (750 REAL, 750 FAKE)
  - **Test (Untouched Benchmark)**: 1,500 images (750 REAL, 750 FAKE)
- **Class Index Mapping**:
  - `0`: FAKE (Synthetic generative faces / Facial manipulation)
  - `1`: REAL (Authentic human photographs from camera sensors)

### Source Attribution:

#### A. NVIDIA Flickr-Faces-HQ (FFHQ)
- **Origin**: NVIDIA Research (Tero Karras et al., *A Style-Based Generator Architecture for Generative Adversarial Networks*, CVPR 2019)
- **Content**: 1024×1024 high-quality genuine human face photographs crawled from Flickr with creative commons licenses.
- **License**: Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0).
- **Usage**: Benchmark baseline for authentic human facial photographs (`REAL`).

#### B. StyleGAN 1-Million Synthetic Faces
- **Origin**: Bojan Tunguz / Kaggle `manjilkarki/deepfake-and-real-images`
- **Content**: Synthetic faces generated by StyleGAN / StyleGAN2 trained on FFHQ.
- **License**: CC BY-SA 4.0 / Research & Educational Permitted.
- **Usage**: Benchmark baseline for fully synthetic generative human faces (`FAKE`).

#### C. DeepGuard Manipulation Benchmark (FaceForensics++)
- **Origin**: FaceForensics++ (Rössler et al., ICCV 2019)
- **Content**: Facial manipulation, face-swap, and reenactment pairs with identity-level separation.
- **License**: Research & Educational Permitted.
- **Usage**: Face-swap and manipulation pairs.

## 2. Video DeepFake Benchmark V1

- **Directory**: `training/datasets/video_deepfake_v1/`
- **Modality**: Video (.mp4, .webm)
- **Provenance**: FaceForensics++ benchmark video clips with uniform frame sampling and facial bounding-box annotations.
- **License**: Permitted for research and educational verification.

---

## 3. Audio & Voice-Cloning Datasets

- **Modality**: Acoustic Audio (.wav, .mp3)
- **Features Extracted**:
  - Spectral Centroid & Bandwidth
  - High-Frequency Energy Ratio (cutoff thresholding)
  - Zero-Crossing Rate (ZCR)
  - Spectral Flatness & Dynamic Range
- **License**: Research Permitted.

---

## 4. Text & Multi-threat Benchmark Datasets

All additional multimodal security modules in TrustGuard AI are backed by verified benchmark datasets and JSON evaluation reports located in `training/reports/`:
- **Phishing URLs**: Verified Kaggle URL classification benchmark (`phishing_urls_training_v3_report.json`)
- **Scam Calls**: Synthetic & recorded phone scam transcript dataset (`scam_calls_training_v2_report.json`)
- **Fake News**: Verified headline & article misinformation dataset (`fake_news_training_v2_report.json`)
- **Email Spam / Spear Phishing**: Enron/SpamAssassin benchmark (`email_spam_training_v3_report.json`)
- **Job Scam**: Employment offer fraud dataset (`job_scam_training_v3_report.json`)
- **Message / SMS Phishing**: Mobile smishing benchmark (`message_spam_training_v3_report.json`)

## 5. Kaggle Authentication & Acquisition Notes

- Kaggle credentials are read directly from local system configuration (`~/.kaggle/kaggle.json`).
- Automated download scripts utilize the official Kaggle CLI.
- No Kaggle API keys or personal credentials are hardcoded or tracked in version control.
